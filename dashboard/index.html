    How to read these numbers
    • Shopify figures are in IST, for complete months from 1 Oct 2025 to 30 Sep 2026. Last year means Oct 2024 to Sep 2025.
    • Net sales = gross sales − discounts − returns. Total sales adds shipping and taxes.
    • Meta includes 8 connected accounts. Grabnix, Oblivion, Natation and Dabster may sell through other storefronts, so MER is shown both ways. "DeoDap accounts" means DeoDap and DeoDap (DIPL) A/c.
    • Platform ROAS relies on each platform's own attribution and double-counts across platforms. MER is the more reliable efficiency number.
    • Google App conversions count app events (installs and in-app actions), so they are far higher than orders.
    • ₹86.3 Cr of net sales has no product title in Shopify (custom, bundled or deleted line items), so the product list covers named products only.
    Data pulled 30 Sep 2026 from Shopify Analytics (ShopifyQL), Windsor.ai (Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Search Console) and GoKwik.
